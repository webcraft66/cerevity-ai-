Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This page explains what information the CerevityAI Partnership Assistant chatbot collects and how it's used.

What we collect

The chatbot may ask for your name, school name, and email address if you engage with it long enough, or ask about pricing or getting in touch. Providing this information is always optional — you can type "skip" at any point to decline.

How it's used

This chatbot has no backend server and does not store, transmit, or process your details anywhere automatically. Instead:

  • Once you provide your name and school, the chatbot generates a pre-filled WhatsApp message containing that information.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere unless you tap the "Send my details on WhatsApp" button and choose to send that message yourself.
  • We only use the information you send us via WhatsApp to follow up about your inquiry regarding a CerevityAI partnership.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.
  • We don't use your information for advertising or marketing unrelated to your inquiry.
  • We don't store your conversation with the chatbot on any server — it exists only in your browser for the duration of your visit.

Voice replies

If you enable voice replies, your browser's built-in text-to-speech feature is used locally on your device. No audio or voice data is recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage only to remember your language and voice-reply preferences on your next visit. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, reach out via the WhatsApp icon on the main chatbot page.
